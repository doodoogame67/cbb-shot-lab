FAQ

What's in here?
Every D1 men's player since 2008, D1-vs-D1 games only, regular season plus postseason, from ESPN box scores and play-by-play (via the sportsdataverse project). Everything is computed in your browser from game-level data, so the date range, conference-only, venue and top-N filters recompute every stat.
Close 2 / Far 2
Same split as T-Rank: Close 2 = layups, dunks and tip-ins; Far 2 = every other 2 (jumpers, floaters, hooks). These come from play-by-play, so they only count games with play-by-play (see the coverage table).
Unassisted
Play-by-play only records assists on makes, so "Unast%" is the share of made shots with no assist. Before 2012 ESPN's play-by-play misses a lot of assists (see the table), which pushes those seasons' unassisted rates up.
2s by distance and corner 3s
Need shot coordinates. ESPN has them for every 2026 shot, about 29% of 2025 shots, 7–24% of 2014–2024 shots, and none before 2014. The "Loc%" column shows each player's coverage. The "rate" columns for distance bands are scaled up from located shots to estimate a share of all FGA.
ORtg, D-Rtg, PRPG
ORtg and D-Rtg use Dean Oliver's individual rating formulas (the Basketball-Reference versions). PRPG here is (ORtg − 88) × usage × Min% × 68 possessions, i.e. points over a replacement player per 68-possession game. T-Rank adjusts its ratings for opponent strength and this site does not, so these won't match T-Rank exactly. BPM is not included.
Role
Estimated from height, assist rate, usage, 3-point rate, rebounding and blocks: Pure PG, Scoring PG, Combo G, Wing G, Wing F, Stretch 4, PF/C, C.
Height, weight, age, class, hometown
ESPN keeps only each player's latest listing, so height and weight are his final listed numbers. Class is worked back from his final class, and age is his age on Feb. 1 of that season (birthdates are missing for many players). BMI = 703 × weight ÷ height².
Top-N teams
Uses the sportsdataverse adjusted-efficiency ranking for that season. It is not venue-adjusted.
Custom filter
The box under the stat filters takes any expression over the column keys (hover a header to see its key), e.g. r_un >= 60 and tpr between 30 and 50 and conf in ('B10','SEC'). It supports and or not, + - * /, between, in (…), and abs min max round.
