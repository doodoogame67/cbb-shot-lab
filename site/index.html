How the numbers work

  • Scope: D1 vs. D1 games only, regular season plus postseason. Percentile shading compares each player to players in the same season with at least 40% of team minutes.
  • Zones: rim = layups, dunks, tip-ins. j2 = every other 2 (jumpers, floaters, hooks), the "non-rim 2s". Located j2s are split into close2 (under 8 ft), mid2 (8–16 ft) and far2 (16 ft to the arc). c3 = corner 3s, ab3 = above-the-break 3s.
  • Unassisted: play-by-play only records assists on made shots, so *_unast is the share of a player's makes in that zone with no assist. *_um40 is unassisted makes per 40 minutes, which rewards volume.
  • Shot locations: ESPN logged coordinates for about 98% of 2026 shots, but only about 26% in 2025 and 5% in 2024. Distance-band columns (close2, mid2, far2, c3, ab3) only cover located shots; loc_cov shows each player's coverage. Rim / j2 / 3 splits come from shot type and are complete every season.
  • Checked against Barttorvik: rim, other-2 and 3PT makes, attempts and percentages match T-Rank exactly for sampled players. Assisted counts can differ by a make or so. Usage and assist rate use Basketball-Reference formulas, so they run a little different from T-Rank's.
  • Query syntax: and, or, not, parentheses, + - * /, comparisons > >= < <= = !=, between 30 and 40, in ('B10','SEC'), and functions abs min max round coalesce. Text compares ignore case. ht is in inches (6'9" = 81).
